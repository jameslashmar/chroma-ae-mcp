# Story, structure and pacing

## The brief

Before anything is built, the brief answers:

- **Intent:** what the viewer feels or knows at the end, in one sentence.
- **Essence:** the core point, the main tension, the emotional centre, the one word to amplify,
  one visual metaphor.
- **Format:** size, fps, length, platform, safe zones, sound on or off by default.
- **Facts:** the approved list of names, dates and numbers that may appear.
- **Arc** (below), **risks**, and **what must not happen**.

Then a beat sheet: for each beat its time range, on-screen text, the hero-frame time (the frame
that has to work as a still), how text enters, locks, is emphasised, exits and bridges to the
next beat, and what would be lost if the motion were removed. Show it and wait for approval.

Treat a short prompt as a seed: expand it into a world, a story, keywords, colour, type, shapes,
camera and sound, but anything the user specified wins. Don't settle on the safe option.

## Arcs

| Length | Arc |
|---|---|
| 10 s | hook, tension, reveal, CTA |
| 15 s | hook, tension, reveal, proof, CTA |
| 30 s | pattern interrupt, conflict, reveal, proof, outcome, CTA |
| Launch, 20-45 s | hook 8 %, tension 12 %, reveal 8 % (placed 20-30 % in), proof 40 %, payoff 20 % (placed 60-80 % in), claim 8 %, end card 8 % (holds 2-3 s) |

Narrative roles for scenes: hook, problem, product intro, key feature, benefits, social proof,
CTA, brand outro. A product promo climbs a ladder: pain, mechanism, proof, confidence, CTA.

**Reference durations:**
- 15 s promo: hook 0-3 s, proof 3-11 s at about 2.5 s a point, end card 11-15 s.
- 45 s launch: 0-4, 4-9, 9-17, 17-22, 22-32, 32-38, 38-45 s; the CTA holds at least 3 s.
- Apple-style product film: 6-8 scenes of about 2.5 s.
- Explainer: 8-14 shots, one sentence each; the question shown big and a first surprise within
  5 s.

## Pacing

- **Hook:** the first readable thing by 0.8-1.2 s (about 0.5 s for an unnarrated sting). The
  first 0-2 s name the first place the eye goes, the largest word or object, and a motion event
  that stops the scroll. The first 2 s are the densest. Frame 0 is the thumbnail: never black,
  never a lone logo.
- **Rhythm:** shots average 1.8-3 s; montage cuts 0.13-0.8 s; a product demo holds one framing
  7-10 s; vertical shots 1-2 s with a new beat every 2-4 s. Hold 0.3-0.75 s before a payoff.
- **Contrast of pace:** fast, stop, explode; dense, release. Alternate high-motion and still
  beats, and don't give every beat the same length. The slowest scene is about three times the
  fastest.
- **Staleness:** something new every 2-4 s. Over 3 s with nothing changing is a dead stretch. A
  static card dies after about 8 s (stage a reveal inside anything over 15 s). Any single
  treatment goes stale after about 6 s. Over a talking head, one card every 6-8 s (under a
  minute), 8-12 s (up to 3 min), 12-20 s (up to 10 min).
- **Pattern interrupt:** past about 2.5 s, change exactly one thing.
- **Holds:** title 0.6-1.2 s after it lands · proof or stat 0.8-1.5 s · CTA at least 1 s and the
  calmest hold in the film · after-state of a transformation at least 1.2 s.
- **Music-led:** every cut, impact, title entrance and camera switch sits on a beat, half beat or
  quarter beat. Write times in beats, not seconds.

## Scene recipes

- **Stat:** the count runs 1.2-1.6 s on a decelerating ease, then holds; a ring or bar finishes
  with the count; the label appears after the number lands (value, then meaning). About 4-6 s in
  all. No overshoot.
- **Logo reveal:** 3-5 s. Vector logos draw on; raster logos reveal through a mask or a scale.
  An underline or light sweep, then hold. Export with alpha for stings.
- **Lower third:** 3-6 s. A bar wipes in from the left (scale X 0 to 100 %), the text slides up
  behind it, hold, then slide out. Bottom 30 %, title-safe, alpha.
- **Highlight a phrase:** the sentence slides up 52 px over 0.8 s (quart out), and after it has
  settled a highlight band sweeps left to right over 0.5-1.5 s scaled to the phrase length. The
  text is never zoomed to emphasise it.
- **Title card (the breather):** one move, then a still hold of 3-5 s.
- **Kinetic beat slam:** one beat list drives every entrance; each phrase gets its own entrance
  (scale slam, side snap, rise and rotate), never the same punch-in reused; hits 0.35-0.6 s,
  exits 0.25 s or less; end on a locked final frame.
- **Press and success:** container scale 0.9 to 1.0 with 5-10 % overshoot over 200 ms, a tick
  drawn over 150 ms starting 100 ms later, 400-500 ms in all.
- **Loading or dashboard build:** skeleton at 0, hero at 100-250 ms, widgets from 200 ms at 60 ms
  apart, chart at 350 ms, all landed by 500 ms, ambient from 650 ms.

## Variants and references

- Build the first 10 s in two or three styles and choose before building the rest.
- Breaking down a reference in five lines: pace, what changes, type, colour and light, the one
  idea. Take moves from a reference video and the look from a still; never copy a logo, copy or
  palette. Note the exact source, at least three observed visual properties and what must not
  drift from the story.
